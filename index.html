Welcome to Camp Half-blood

Camp Half-blood is a designated place for all demigods and mythical creatures. You ask what a demigod is? Well a demigod is a spawn of both a mortal and a god. Here at Camp Half-blood we foster those children of Greek Gods and Goddesses. In a effort to help protect children of the olympian gods and fight against the evils of Tartarus. This is probably all a lot to take in so I will let you explore the site and get comfortable

If you click "Camp Ground" you can get an overview of the camp grounds. This will help you naivgate around and prevent you from getting lost and/or captured by those pesky Ares kids

If you click "Merch" you will be able to purchase gear, clothes, and enchanted equipment from the local traders. The money you spend here will be used as offering to the gods to keep Camp Half-blood protected.

Clicking "About" will help answer any additional questions that you may have. Also, it will provide you with more information about the establishment of Camp Halfblood.
