About Camp Half-blood

It is a training ground where young demigods learn to fight mythical monsters and develop their heroic abilities through activities like quests, chariot races, and combat training. Camp Half-Blood started in ancient Greece, with Chiron initially establishing it at the foot of Mount Pelion. It relocated to the base of Mount Olympus and has since moved with the Western world's civilization. It is a sanctuary for demigods, first opened to train them in ancient Greece, with famous figures like George Washington being among the campers throughout history.
